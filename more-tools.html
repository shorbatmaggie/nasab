Kān Zamān

Kān Zamān is a date converter for academics working on the Islamicate world. Users can convert an entire year range or a specific day using either Arabic-Indic and Western Arabic numerals as input (e.g. ١٢٣٤ and 1234). Converted dates display in English, IJMES transliteration, and Arabic.

Shorba Tools

Shorba is a suite of visualization and analysis apps for researchers. In addition to Nasab, Shorba includes the following:

  • Minhāj: a searchable database of over 30,000 digitized Arabic texts. Browse texts or find what you're looking for using utilities like exact match, fuzzy search, normalized queries that account for orthographic variation, root and lemma search, and clitic-aware search (e.g. search results for "كتاب" will also include words like كتاب، الكتاب، بالكتاب، كتابهن).
    • Naẓar: a visualization and annotation tool for comparing and analyzing Islamic legal texts. You can upload or import sources, sort them by subject, and display them chronologically to track development over time. Built-in tools include side-by-side text comparision (diffViewer), text reuse detection (passim), and recogito tools like multi-color highlighting, note-taking, and tagging. Then export your notes or sync them to airtable.
