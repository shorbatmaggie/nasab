Kān Zamān

Kān Zamān is a date converter for academics working on the Islamicate world. Users can convert an entire year range or a specific day using either Arabic-Indic and Western Arabic numerals as input (e.g. ١٢٣٤ and 1234). Converted dates display in English, IJMES transliteration, and Arabic.
